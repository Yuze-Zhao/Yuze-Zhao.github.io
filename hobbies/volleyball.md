Volleyball is such a wonderful sport! My advisor likes to say that playing volleyball is a lot like doing research—you need solid fundamentals, good technique, teamwork, strategy, and plenty of perseverance. For me, volleyball has not only kept me active but also helped me make friends and share great moments together.
